# EasyMotor Demo — Interactive Effects

These four effects let you feel different mechanical behaviors created by motor control.

| Effect | What it means | What you feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spring Return** | A simulated spring that pulls the handle back to its starting position. | Turn the handle away from where the effect started. The farther you turn, the stronger the restoring force. Release it, and it returns toward that position. |
| **Viscous Damper** | Smooth resistance, similar to moving a paddle through thick oil. | Turn slowly for light resistance; turn faster for stronger resistance. There is no preferred position or spring-back action. |
| **Detent Knob** | A simulated rotary selector with distinct “click” positions. | Turn the handle to feel it settle into evenly spaced positions, **15° apart**. These clicks are created electronically, without mechanical notches. |
| **Virtual Flywheel** | A motor-assisted spinning effect inspired by a flywheel. | Give the handle a push, and the motor captures the motion and keeps it turning after you let go. A stronger push can increase the speed, up to the demo limit. The motor actively maintains rotation, so it can keep spinning instead of simply coasting to a stop. |

Select an effect and click **Start Selected Effect**. Use the demo’s external handle with the motor unloaded. Click **STOP** to end the effect.
